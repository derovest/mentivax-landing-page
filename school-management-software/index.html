Start free pilot Book a demo

One system for the whole school, not one app per department.

In many schools the fee counter has one Excel file, the class teacher has a register, the office has another file for certificates and the accountant has Tally. None of them talk to each other.

School management software brings all of it into one place. A student is entered once at admission, and the same record carries their fees, attendance, marks, transport and certificates through every year they are in the school. A fee paid at the counter shows in the parent portal, the day book and the accounts at once.

That alone saves time. But a system that only stores data still needs people to act on it, which is where most school software stops and where Mentivax keeps going.

How to choose school management software

Before comparing feature lists, ask what will actually change in your office on a normal Tuesday.

  • Will it reduce work, or just move it? If staff still compile reminders and reports by hand, the software has digitized the work but not reduced it.
  • Can your least technical staff member use it? Ask them to try a real task in the demo, not watch a presentation.
  • Can you start small? Paying for 20 modules on day one usually means using three of them.
  • Who moves your data? Re-typing years of fee records is where many software projects stall.
  • How do parents receive information? In India that usually means WhatsApp, not another app to install.
  • Who controls sensitive actions? Messages about fees and marks should go out only with the approval you decide.

School management software: common questions

What does school management software do?

School management software keeps a school’s records and daily operations in one system: student records and admissions, fees and receipts, attendance, exams and report cards, timetable, staff and payroll, transport and parent communication. Instead of separate registers and Excel files, every department works from the same data.

How is Mentivax different from other school management systems?

Most school management systems store and organise data, and staff still do the follow-up by hand. Mentivax includes the same modules, then automates the routine work on top of them: fee reminders, receipts and absence alerts go out on their own, and an AI agent answers questions and prepares work for staff to approve.

Do we have to buy every module?

No. Mentivax has 23 modules and you switch on only the ones you need. Many schools start with fees or attendance and add more later. You only pay for the modules you use.

Is it suitable for a small school?

Yes. Mentivax is built for small and medium schools that don’t have an IT team. Staff can type what they need in plain words instead of learning dozens of menus, and we set everything up for you.

Can parents use it without installing an app?

Yes. Parents get reminders, receipts, alerts and circulars on WhatsApp, and open the parent portal in any phone browser with their mobile number. There is no account to create and no app to download.

How long does it take to start?

Most schools can go live within a week. We import your existing data from Excel, CSV or your old software and check it row by row, so your staff carry on from where they left off.
