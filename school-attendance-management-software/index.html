Taking attendance is quick. Everything after it isn’t.

Marking a register takes a few minutes. Totalling it, calling parents, chasing repeated absences and preparing the monthly report takes far longer.

With a paper register, a child who misses school three days in a row is often noticed only when someone happens to look back through the pages. Parents find out late. At month end, a teacher or office assistant totals each row by hand and copies the numbers into a report.

Mentivax makes marking faster, but the bigger change is what happens next: parents are alerted automatically, repeated absences are flagged, and the monthly register is ready when the month ends. It’s one part of Mentivax’s school management software, and a good example of school automation in practice.

Attendance management: common questions

How do teachers mark attendance in Mentivax?

Teachers mark each child with one tap, or mark the whole class present in one go and change the few who are absent. The teacher app works offline, so registers can be taken even where the network is weak.

Are parents told automatically when their child is absent?

Yes. When a student is marked absent, Mentivax can send the parent a WhatsApp absence alert automatically. You decide whether alerts go out straight away or after a staff member checks the register.

Can Mentivax flag students who are absent repeatedly?

Yes. You can set a rule such as “when a student is absent three days running, message the parent and inform the class teacher”. Early-warning flags also show students whose attendance is slipping over time.

Can we get the monthly attendance register without Excel?

Yes. The monthly register is built from daily marking, with attendance percentages for every student, and can be exported to Excel if you still need a copy.

Does it work with biometric or card readers?

Mentivax supports card, fingerprint and QR readers at the school gate and on the bus, as well as photo-assisted attendance with parent consent. None of this is required: most schools start with the teacher app.

Can we track staff attendance too?

Yes. Staff attendance and leave, with leave balances, are part of the staff module and feed into payroll.
