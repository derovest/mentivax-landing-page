Your own AI agent

An assistant that knows your school.

Mentivax is more than software you fill in. Every school gets a personal AI agent and workflows that run on their own, so routine work gets done without anyone chasing it.

The Mentivax Agent

Ask in plain words and follow up like you would with a colleague. The agent prepares fee reminders in English or Tamil, substitute cover, leave applications, PTM bookings, worksheets and reports, each with an exact preview for you to approve. Staff can even ask it on WhatsApp.

Workflows that run themselves

“When a child is absent three days running, message the parent.” Write a rule as a sentence and Mentivax turns it into a workflow that runs automatically, as a suggestion, or after an approval. Fee reminders, receipts and absence alerts go out on time, every time.

Copilots for teachers and parents

Teachers get lesson plans, question papers, answer-sheet marking and report-card remarks drafted for them to check. Parents ask about their own child in English or Tamil and get answers from the school’s records.

AI drafts, a person decides. Nothing involving money, marks or families happens without approval, and the agent only does what the person using it is allowed to do.

One automation tool for the whole school

Every school job, in one place. Automated.

Mentivax replaces the registers, Excel files and separate apps your school runs on today, then automates the work on top of them. Switch on only the modules you need.
