Fee collection shouldn’t depend on one person and one Excel file.

In many schools the fee office runs on a spreadsheet that one person understands, a receipt book and a lot of phone calls.

Balances drift when a payment is entered twice or not at all. Parents call for receipt copies. Every few weeks someone filters the sheet for pending fees, types reminders and calls the families who haven’t responded. At month end the same person builds the collection report again, by hand.

Mentivax replaces the spreadsheet with a fee system where every rupee is billed, collected and accounted for, and then automates the follow-up so that reminders, receipts and reports no longer wait for someone to find time.

Fee management: common questions

What is school fee management software?

School fee management software records each student’s fees, collects payments, issues receipts and tracks what is still pending. Mentivax also automates the follow-up: reminders to parents with pending fees, receipts on WhatsApp and the daily collection report.

Can we set up instalment, term and monthly fees?

Yes. Fee structures support instalments, splits across fee heads and transport fares, so each class can be billed the way your school actually charges, whether by term, by month or in instalments.

Can parents pay online?

Yes. Parents can pay through an online payment link and the receipt is written automatically. Payments at the counter can be split across cash, UPI, bank and card in one receipt.

Are fee reminders sent automatically?

They can be. You decide whether reminders go out automatically on the schedule you set, or are prepared for a staff member to review and approve first. Reminders are sent on WhatsApp in English or Tamil.

How do concessions and scholarships work?

Concessions go through an approval step before they apply, and scholarships, late fees, credit and refunds are recorded against the student’s account, so the balance is always correct.

Can we still give our auditor the accounts?

Yes. Fee collections flow into the day book and accounts, and Mentivax exports to Tally for your auditor.

Can we use only fee management?

Yes. Many schools start with fee management alone because it saves the most time. You can add attendance, exams or other modules later, and only pay for what you use.
