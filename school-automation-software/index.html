What is school automation software?

School automation software does the repetitive follow-up work that school records create, so office staff and teachers don’t have to.

Most schools that “went digital” moved their registers and Excel sheets into software. The data now lives on a screen, but the work around it hasn’t changed. Someone still checks who hasn’t paid, types the reminder, calls the parent of the child who has been absent all week, and copies numbers into a month-end report.

Automation software closes that gap. It watches the records your school already keeps, applies the rules you set, and either does the routine task itself or prepares it for a person to approve. The goal is simple: fewer jobs that depend on one person remembering to do them.

Mentivax is built this way from the ground up. It is complete school management software (admissions, fees, attendance, exams, timetable, transport, payroll and parent communication) with workflows and a personal AI agent working across all of it.

How the work gets done for you.

Automation in Mentivax isn’t one feature. It runs through every module, so a fee paid, a register marked or a leave approved can set the next step in motion.

One connected system underneath

Automation works because the data is in one place. A fee paid at the counter updates the balance, the parent portal, the day book and the accounts at once, so the next step can start on its own.

Automation with a person in charge.

AI drafts, a person decides. Automation should remove busywork, not judgement.

Every Mentivax workflow runs in one of three ways, and you choose which:

  • Automatically, for routine messages such as receipts and same-day absence alerts.
  • As a suggestion, shown to the right staff member to act on, such as substitute cover for a teacher on leave.
  • After approval, for anything involving money, marks or families, such as fee reminders to overdue parents.

The AI agent only does what the person using it is allowed to do, and nothing it prepares is sent until someone approves the exact preview. Children’s names are removed before anything reaches the AI, every change is recorded in an audit log, and each AI feature can be switched off.

School automation: common questions

What is school automation software?

School automation software takes over the repetitive work that sits around school records: sending fee reminders, alerting parents about absences, writing receipts, compiling reports and following up on pending tasks. A traditional school management system stores the data. Automation software also acts on it, either on its own or by preparing the work for a staff member to approve.

Is school automation software different from school management software or a school ERP?

They overlap. School management software and school ERPs keep the records: students, fees, attendance, exams, staff. Automation is the layer that does the follow-up work on those records. Mentivax includes the full set of school management modules and adds workflows and an AI agent on top, so the same system that holds your fee records can also send the reminders.

Will automation send messages to parents without anyone checking?

Only if you choose that. Each workflow can run automatically, appear as a suggestion, or wait for approval. Most schools let routine messages such as receipts and absence alerts go out on their own, and keep anything involving money, marks or a sensitive conversation behind an approval step. Nothing the AI agent prepares is sent until a person approves it.

Do we need technical staff to set up automations?

No. A workflow is written as a sentence, such as “when a student is absent three days running, message the parent”, and Mentivax turns it into a rule. Our team also sets up your first workflows with you during onboarding.

Which school tasks should we automate first?

Start with the job your office repeats most often and finds most frustrating. For most schools that is fee follow-up: finding who has not paid, reminding parents and writing receipts. Absence alerts and monthly attendance reports are usually next.

Does Mentivax use AI for automation?

Workflows follow the rules you set; they do not need AI. The Mentivax Agent uses AI to answer questions in plain words and to prepare work such as reminders, reports, substitute cover and worksheets for a person to approve. Children’s names are removed before anything reaches the AI, and each AI feature can be switched off.
